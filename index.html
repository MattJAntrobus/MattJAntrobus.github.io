    I am a fourth year PhD student at the University of Manchester, supervised by Prof. Peter Symonds. I am mainly interested in the intersection of Homological algebra and Representation Theory. In particular, I have been working on understanding the first Hochschild cohomology of finite group algebras, trying to prove the conjectures of Markus Linckelmann.
